About

Technical depth. Commercial judgement. Accountable delivery.

I'm Eleftherios Kathijotes, a London-based technology and programme leader. I deliver complex enterprise implementations, recover initiatives that have lost momentum, and improve how delivery works afterwards.

My work spans enterprise software, digital commerce and education technology. The constant is a demanding integration, teams with competing priorities, and a commercial outcome that depends on all of it landing together. I hold the technical detail and the business case in view at once, and make the trade-offs between them explicit.

At Forter I lead technical delivery for strategic enterprise accounts. In my first eleven months I completed a group of multi-year stalled projects, unlocking $1.5 million in revenue. The work turned on establishing what each programme required and who owned the decisions still outstanding.

Earlier roles taught me where durable improvement comes from. At Coursera I delivered more than seventy enterprise projects and automated a scaled-account deployment task from twenty minutes to under ten seconds. At Instructure I led four consultants and helped raise team satisfaction to 90%.

Computer science at Kingston taught me to read a system rather than trust its description. Management at UCL taught me to judge what is worth building, and when an organisation is ready for it. I am now exploring how applied AI can reduce administrative effort and support programme coordination, with ownership and human review designed in.

I hold voluntary directorships at Granville Square Ltd and South City Court RTM, contributing to financial scrutiny, oversight of building works, and coordination with managing agents and residents. Decisions rest with the board collectively.
